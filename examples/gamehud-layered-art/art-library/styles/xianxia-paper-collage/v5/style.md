## 继承的风格文字

以用户偏好的v1原图为参考，保留暖奶油纸白、青绿、朱砂红与少量暖金，纸张纤维、叠层浅浮雕、柔和接触阴影、正面略俯视、低矮圆形纸艺底座。五个功能主体与底座使用一致的材质、配色、光照和视角。

## 本版融合方向

Preserve reference's layered-paper depth and comfortable muted palette. Blend elegant restrained gongbi thin contour lines and subtle flat color layering into paper shapes. Graceful slightly elongated scroll, pavilion and cranes, very fine but sparse floral motifs, less heavy red area and more cream paper breathing space. Low round bases retain matching paper-cut cloud motifs. Avoid photorealism, heavy gold filigree and busy ornament.
