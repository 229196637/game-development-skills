## 继承的风格文字

以用户偏好的v1原图为参考，保留暖奶油纸白、青绿、朱砂红与少量暖金，纸张纤维、叠层浅浮雕、柔和接触阴影、正面略俯视、低矮圆形纸艺底座。五个功能主体与底座使用一致的材质、配色、光照和视角。

## 本版融合方向

Keep reference's Chinese fantasy paper-cut visual identity, shallow layered paper and teal-cinnabar-cream palette. Explore cleaner graphic two-tone flat shading, fewer planes and simpler larger cloud/petal patterns, more deliberate silhouette design for small game UI. Preserve enough tactile paper grain and subtle contact shadows to feel comfortable, not a generic flat vector icon. Objects and simple low round bases share line weight and material. Avoid neon colors, plastic 3D shine, excessive gradients and intricate micro-details.

## 实际读图
