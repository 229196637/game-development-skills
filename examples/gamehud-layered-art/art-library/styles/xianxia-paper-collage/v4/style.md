## 本版融合方向

Keep the reference's tactile paper collage and muted teal-cinnabar-cream palette. Make shapes slightly more rounded and silhouettes simpler, charming light cartoon proportions WITHOUT big-headed chibi deformation. Use fewer larger decorative cut-paper shapes, clean gently curved edges, gentle shallow-relief shading. Elegant cranes remain anatomically legible, subjects and bases seamlessly share paper materials. Avoid plastic toys and exaggerated baby proportions.
