## 继承的风格文字

以用户偏好的v1原图为参考，保留暖奶油纸白、青绿、朱砂红与少量暖金，纸张纤维、叠层浅浮雕、柔和接触阴影、正面略俯视、低矮圆形纸艺底座。五个功能主体与底座使用一致的材质、配色、光照和视角。

## 本版融合方向

Keep reference's layered-paper relief but soften cut edges with pale watercolor washes and gentle mineral pigment shading. Airy cream and muted celadon, light cinnabar accents, visible delicate paper fibers, restrained fine patterns, slightly reduced ornament density. Soft diffused daylight. Do not turn into a monochrome ink painting; keep solid readable layered subjects and round paper bases.
